**Tip:** PropCo also has its own step-by-step walkthroughs inside the app. Open **Step-by-step guides** in the menu and it ticks off each step as you do it.

## How to read the guides

- **Bold words** are buttons, menu items or screen names exactly as they appear.
- *The menu* is the dark panel on the left on a computer. On a phone it is the bottom bar plus **More**.
- What you can see depends on your role. If a screen in this guide is missing from your menu, ask your administrator (see [Team and security](10-team-and-security.md)).
- Screenshots use invented demo data (a company called Mwangaza Property Managers).
