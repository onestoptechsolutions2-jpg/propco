# 12. Troubleshooting and FAQ

## Signing in

**I forgot my password.** Ask your company administrator to reset it (Team and roles, open your name, Reset password). If you are the only administrator, contact PropCo support. There is no "forgot password" link yet.

**"Your account is locked."** Five wrong passwords lock an account for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask your administrator to reset your password.

**"This account has been suspended."** An administrator suspended you, or PropCo suspended the whole company (for example for non-payment). Contact your administrator or PropCo support.

**A menu item is missing.** Your role does not include it. Ask your administrator to change your role or add a custom role (see [Team and security](10-team-and-security.md)).

## Rent and payments

**A tenant shows Late but says they paid.** Check **Confirm M-Pesa** for a waiting proof, and the tenant's payment history. If the money is real, press **Mark paid**.

**"That code was already used."** Each M-Pesa code works once. Check whether the payment was already recorded.

**An online payment is stuck on Pending.** The tenant may not have entered their PIN yet. M-Pesa prompts expire after a few minutes; PropCo checks again by itself and marks it failed if it never completes. Send a new request if needed.

**The tenant paid online but the rent is not marked paid.** Wait a couple of minutes; PropCo also checks with the provider every 2 minutes. If it still does not appear, record it by hand and tell your administrator (see [payments troubleshooting](../payments.md#troubleshooting)).

**The owner payout looks wrong.** Payouts use rent marked **Paid** and due in that month. Record any missed payment and press **Recalculate**. A payout already **Paid** is never changed.

## Messages

**Messages say "Skipped".** The person has no phone or email on file, or your PropCo does not have an email or SMS provider set up. WhatsApp messages never send by themselves: tap **Send on WhatsApp**.

**WhatsApp opens but the number is wrong.** Check the phone number on the tenant's page. Kenyan numbers like `0712 345 678` and `+254 712 345 678` both work.

## Chat

**Chat says it did not understand.** Chat matches keywords. Include the tenant's name or the unit and an amount, like `John paid rent 25000 mpesa`. Type `Help` for examples, or use the normal screens.

**Chat found the wrong tenant.** It never saves until you tap, so tap **Cancel** and retype with the full name or the unit.

## Phone

**The page looks out of date.** Pages refresh by themselves when something changes, but not while you are typing or the tab is hidden. Pull down to refresh.

**I cannot install the app.** On an iPhone use Safari, Share, **Add to Home Screen**. On Android use Chrome and the **Install app** banner.

## FAQ

**Who can see my data?** Only people in your company. Each company's data is kept completely separate. PropCo support can see settings and counts to help you, not your tenants' details or finances, and everything they do on your account appears in your activity log.

**Does PropCo hold my tenants' money?** No. Online payments go straight to your own M-Pesa or Flutterwave account.

**Can tenants sign in?** Not yet. Staff, landlords and owners can.

**Can I export my data?** Books has a CSV download. Receipts, invoices, statements and payslips can be printed or saved as PDF. Ask PropCo support for anything else.

**What happens if I stop paying?** You move to the Free plan (5 units). Features such as utilities and payroll switch off, and nothing is deleted.

**Is there a Swahili version?** Not yet.

**Where do I get help?** Ask your administrator first. For PropCo itself, use the contact details on your **Plan and billing** page.
